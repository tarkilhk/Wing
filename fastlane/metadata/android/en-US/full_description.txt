Continue conversations with your own Hermes Agent from an Android phone.

Wing is an independent Android client. It requires a compatible Hermes dashboard and Desktop Gateway that you operate or have permission to use. It does not run a model on the phone or provide a hosted AI account. Your server and model provider may have their own costs.

Features:
- Streaming chats with per-chat model and reasoning choices
- Profiles, projects, pinned chats, unread filters and conversation search
- Tool activity, subagent and goal supervision where supported by the server
- Durable unsent drafts, text/file follow-up queues and text-only steering
- Photo/file attachments, reviewed Android shares and voice dictation into a draft
- Output reading and sharing, including images, PDFs, Markdown and supported diagrams
- Supported profile/server administration and device appearance settings

Local notifications require an active client connection and have limited coverage of unopened chats. Follow-up queues drain while the app is running and connected. If Android closes during a send, check server history before resending a recovered draft.

Use HTTPS or an encrypted private network for remote connections. Server capabilities determine which operations are available. There is no offline conversation archive, general remote filesystem browser or Cron/messaging administration.

Read the setup guide and privacy policy in the project repository. The privacy policy is also available offline in App settings.
