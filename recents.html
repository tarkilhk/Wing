Recent chats and ongoing work

Recents shows chats with messages from the last 24 hours alongside ongoing work, across profiles on your current connection. Older conversations are still available in Chats.

Find the chats that need you

Choose Running to check work in progress, or Needs input to find conversations waiting for a response. All shows the full Recents list.

For example, leave a research task running while you work on a launch plan in another profile. Check Needs input when you are ready to respond.

Open a chat in its profile

Tap a conversation to open it with the right profile selected. Go back to return to Recents and check the next chat.

You can also open Recents from Wing's Android launcher shortcut menu.
