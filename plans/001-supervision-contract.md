# Captured supervision

One workspace-mounted ProfileSupervisionSession captures chat/runtime and borrows
canonical workspace facts. Goal/subagent/background panels render detached typed
observations and forward intent. Criteria reviews capture content and one-based
protocol index; editors retain their own close lifetime. Subagent detail windows
own the two-second tail cadence, three-failure stop/Retry, last available output
and steer/interrupt feedback. Existing canonical roster/control/process state
remains with the workspace; no mirrored cache is introduced.

Required physical control dispatch predicates revoke unsent actions when the
captured owner/editor/detail retires. Already admitted actions keep their original
ACK/readback semantics. Reentrant listener retirement suppresses publication and
releases notifiers after notification unwinds. Workspace closes the session before
its borrowed controller. Six panel compositions share that one session.
